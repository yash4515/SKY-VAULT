1. Purpose and Operational Scope

SKY-VAULT is an air-gapped cryptographic software framework providing post-quantum document distribution, zero-leak in-memory forensic watermarking, and tamper-evident audit logging. Deploying and operating the software constitutes agreement to these technical conditions.

2. Mathematical Scope of Cryptographic Attribution

Forensic verification reports generated by SKY-VAULT are subject to strict evidentiary bounds:

  • Credential Authentication Binding: A report of "CRYPTOGRAPHICALLY VERIFIED" mathematically certifies that the specific recipient private key signed a canonical decryption event record matching the recovered watermark and hash digests.
  • Physical Attribution Limitation: Cryptographic attribution establishes credential authentication. It does not provide judicial proof of physical human intent, coercion, device theft, or physical camera capture beyond the registered recipient credential boundary.
  • Probabilistic Collusion Resistance: Tardos fingerprinting codes provide provable error bounds up to the configured colluder threshold (c). Exceeding the design colluder threshold may result in an INCONCLUSIVE or MULTIPLE CANDIDATES forensic result state.

3. Recipient Credential Obligations

Authorized recipients registered within the SKY-VAULT directory must maintain strict custody over credential material:

  • Key Protection: Recipients must safeguard private signing keys within approved hardware tokens or designated secure enclaves. Sharing or exporting key material is strictly prohibited.
  • Revocation Reporting: Any credential compromise must be reported immediately to the organizational root Certificate Authority for inclusion in the Certificate Revocation List (CRL).
  • Decryption Authorization: Decryption requests must be initiated solely for legitimate organizational tasks within approved air-gapped viewing environments.

4. Post-Quantum Cryptographic Standards Compliance

The cryptographic pipeline adheres to standardized post-quantum algorithms published by NIST:

  • Key Encapsulation: NIST FIPS 203 ML-KEM-768 for asymmetric document key encapsulation.
  • Digital Signatures: NIST FIPS 204 ML-DSA-65 for deterministic event signing.
  • Bulk Encryption: AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with unique 96-bit initialization vectors.
  • Hash Algorithms: SHA3-256 and SHAKE-256 for deterministic canonical serialization and digest generation.

5. Air-Gapped Network Licensing and Integrity

This software is authorized for installation within isolated organizational boundaries. Tampering with the hash-chain ledger, bypassing the in-memory watermarking pipeline, or modifying forensic verification checks violates the operational license and invalidates all generated evidentiary claims.
